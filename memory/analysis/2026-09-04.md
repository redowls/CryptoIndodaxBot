## Structural findings (both new, both real-money relevant)

**1. The ledger reports GROSS P&L — there is no fee term anywhere.**
`ledger.py:63` is `round((exit_price - entry_price) * qty, 2)`. Indodax charges 0.2% taker
per side; today that is **Rp1.320, i.e. 21.5% of the day's realized loss**, invisible in
`trades.json`. This is the same defect CryptoAutoBot carried until IMP-B01. Worse here:
`risk.circuit_breaker_tripped()` sums the same gross `pnl` field, so the 4%-of-equity halt
under-measures true drawdown by ~20%. Not close to tripping today (gross −1.2% of equity),
but the accounting is wrong in the direction that delays protection. **Not patched — this
is the analysis routine; flagging for the improvement pass.**

**2. The notional cap binds, so per-trade risk is ~⅓ of the design value.**
`position_size` caps notional at `equity / MAX_POSITIONS` = Rp166.7k. Intended risk is
`RISK_PCT 1.5% × Rp500k = Rp7.500`; the cap cut BTC's qty from 0.000326 to 0.000117, so
**actual risk was Rp2.694 (0.54% of equity)**. Consequence that matters more than the
number: once the cap binds, fees are a *fixed* 0.4% × Rp166.7k ≈ Rp667 per round trip
regardless of stop width, so **fee-drag-as-a-share-of-1R = 0.4% / stop%** — 25% for BTC
(1.61% stop), 21% for ETH (1.94%). The low-ATR majors are the structurally worst
instruments on this account, and they are exactly the two that traded. Sharpens the
09-01 fee note: it is not position size that decides fee drag, it is ATR%.

Note `policy.max_positions` only limits *slots* (`trader.py:238`); sizing always divides by
`config.MAX_POSITIONS`, so lowering it in policy is purely cautious with no sizing side
effect. Verified before setting 2.
