## TOP FINDING — execution lag costs ~4x what fees do, and nothing models it

The 09-06 finding (`avg_fill_price` sends a bare numeric order id, Indodax
rejects it `[1109]`, so the ledger stores the snapshot 1H close instead of the
fill) is **still unfixed** after today's engine change. Verified live again:

    avg_fill_price('LINK', '64053754')               -> None
    avg_fill_price('LINK', 'linkidr-market-64053754') -> 231.478

Pulling the real fills for today's LTC round trip via the prefixed id, and
cross-checking against the IDR cash deltas in `logs/trader.log`, ties out to
the rupiah:

| | ledger says | exchange says | cash delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| LTC buy  | 958.000   | 976.000 (fee Rp270)   | −Rp128.360 ✓ |
| LTC sell | 1.034.000 | 1.024.000 (fee Rp564) | +Rp133.826 ✓ |
| P&L | **+Rp9.974** | **+Rp5.466** | 82% overstated |

The decomposition is the new part. Fees were Rp834 = **0.63% round trip**,
exactly the `OBSERVED_ROUND_TRIP_PCT` the engine now filters on. The other
**2.85%** is decision-to-execution lag, and the hourly closes show the
mechanism cleanly:

    09:00 bar close   958.000   <- the bar the entry decision was made on
    09:14 fill        976.000   (+1.88%)
    10:00 bar close   982.000
    ...
    13:00 bar close 1.034.000   <- the spike the TP fired on
    13:14 fill      1.024.000   (−0.97%)
    14:00 bar close 1.007.000

The snapshot cron runs at :07 and the trader at :14, so every order executes
~14 minutes after the close it was evaluated on — **in the direction of the
move that triggered it**. Entries fire on strength and fill higher; TP exits
fire after a spike and fill into the mean-reversion. This is adverse selection
by construction, not book impact. Same signature on the other fills: DOT entry
+0.95% (17.152 recorded vs 17.316 true), LINK entry +0.42%, LINK's 09-06 exit
−1.43% (233.000 recorded vs 229.670 true); only AVAX, entered on a quiet
02:14 candle, came in flat at −0.03%.

Consequence for today's engine change: LTC "passed" `MIN_ATR_PCT` at a modelled
13% drag, and its true all-in cost was **3.48% against a 4.86% stop = 72% of
1R**. The new floor is calibrated on the 0.63% fee term while the larger cost
term is unmodelled and unmeasured. `MIN_ATR_PCT` is directionally right and
should stay; the number behind it is optimistic by roughly 5x on the names that
move most.
