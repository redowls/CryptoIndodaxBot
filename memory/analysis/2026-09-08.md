## FINDING 1 — the re-entry throttle cannot fire after a stop on any position held >24h

AVAX **stopped out and re-bought itself 25 seconds later**, in the same cycle:

```
exit_time  2026-09-08T04:14:04   reason stop
entry_time 2026-09-08T04:14:29   (same coin, same qty)
```

Real fills (`/api/v2/myTrades?symbol=avaxidr`, prefixed ids):

```
09-08 04:14 SELL px=141237 qty=0.9268627  quote=130907 fee=549
09-08 04:14 BUY  px=142292 qty=0.92722008 quote=131935 fee=277
```

The ledger records **141196 for both legs** (the shared snapshot close), so it
shows a clean, costless handoff. Reality: sold at 141237 and re-bought at
142292 — paid **Rp1.055/unit (+0.75%) more to hold the same coin**, plus
Rp826 of commissions. **Total ≈ Rp1.804 burned for zero change in exposure**,
0.34% of equity.

Root cause, and it is one line: `ledger.throttled()` (`ledger.py:82-88`) reads
`led["last_entry_attempt"]`, which is written only at entry
(`trader.py:141` via `record_entry_attempt`). **The throttle window starts at
ENTRY, not at EXIT.** AVAX entered 09-07 02:14 and stopped 09-08 04:14 — 26.0h
later, so its 24h window had *already expired at the moment the stop fired*,
and the same cycle's entry loop re-bought it. `REENTRY_THROTTLE_HOURS = 24` is
therefore structurally incapable of imposing any cooldown on exactly the trades
that most need one: every position held longer than the throttle window.

The fix is to key the throttle off exit time (or stamp `record_entry_attempt`
on close). Until then the cooldown has to live in `blocked_symbols`.

Immediate consequence for tomorrow: **LINK's throttle expired 09-08 04:14**,
nine hours before its stop. It has zero cooldown and can be re-bought the
instant its 1H stack flips UP.
