## Main work today: what the policy layer has actually cost

`blocked by policy` is the **second-largest rejection bucket in the log — 420 coin-hours**,
behind only fee drag (435) and ahead of ADX (320). That layer is my own output, so I
replayed all 420 against the stored hourly snapshots (11 days, 270 files), reconstructing
extras from consecutive snapshots (1H closes) and per-day last 1D closes (day change).

**Result: 385 of 420 blocked coin-hours (92%) were inert** — the coin would have been
rejected one step later anyway (86 fee drag, 84 ADX, 68 1H MIXED, 42 not-green, 39 1H DOWN,
26 late entry, 22 RSI band). Only **35 coin-hours across 10 episodes were binding**, and
they concentrate in exactly two names: DOT 18h, UNI 15h, LTC 2h.

Forward-testing those 10 binding episodes through the real `check_exit` (entry at the
first 1H-UP-stack close of the episode, 3.0xATR stop, 2.0xATR trail, TP 2.5R, hourly
closes, 0.63% round-trip fees):

| lag assumption | total | outcomes |
|---|---:|---|
| ideal fills (snapshot closes) | **+2.26R** | 3 TP / 7 stop |
| measured 1.08% adverse fill | **−0.00R** | 3 TP / 7 stop |

The 1.08% is the mean of the three entry lags actually measured on real fills
(DOT +0.95%, LTC +1.88%, LINK +0.42% — the 09-07 insight), applied adversely to both legs.

So: **the trades my blocks prevented were worth exactly nothing.** The entire apparent
+2.26R benefit of not blocking is consumed by the execution lag the engine does not model.
And the shape is the familiar one — the three winners (UNI 09-03 +2.61R, DOT 09-07 +2.56R,
DOT 09-08 +2.34R) sit in or beside the same 09-06/09-07 rally that contains all of the
account's real profit; the other seven bleed. A 30% win rate carried entirely by one rally,
in a book of trades the bot never took — the counterfactual has the *same* no-edge signature
as the live ledger, which is itself informative: the block layer is not what is wrong.

Caveats, stated plainly: entries approximated at the first 1H-UP hour of the episode rather
than an hour-by-hour re-evaluation; exits on 1H closes only (no intrabar high/low), which
flatters stops; `max_positions` and capital ignored, and several episodes overlap in time
(09-06 DOT+UNI, 09-07 DOT+UNI) so not all were simultaneously takeable. Directionally the
conclusion is robust — it survives at both lag settings.

Today's DOT block was one of the binding ones (3 hours) and it did save money: DOT fell
4.29% and both lag settings score that episode −1.24R / −1.81R. That is one trade, not
evidence.
