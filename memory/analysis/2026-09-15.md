## The cost reconciliation (the finding that matters)

Closing the books against the accounting identity rather than the ledger:

| | |
|---|---|
| true marked equity | **Rp501.836** (= `logs/trader.log` 16:14 to the rupiah) |
| funded | Rp500.861 |
| unrealized XRP (net of entry fee) | −Rp2.447 |
| **net realized, lifetime** | **+Rp3.422** |
| gross realized (ledger `pnl` sum) | +Rp30.336 |
| **overstatement** | **8.9x** |
| total cost drag on Rp3.40M turnover | Rp26.914 |

**Implied all-in round trip = 1.58%.** The engine assumes 0.63%
(`OBSERVED_ROUND_TRIP_PCT`), and `MIN_ATR_PCT` is derived from it:
`0.63 / MAX_FEE_DRAG_R 0.28 / STOP_ATR_MULT 3.0 = 0.75%`.

This is independently corroborated: 09-11 measured a **1.08% adverse fill** on
real orders (DOT +0.95%, LTC +1.88%, LINK +0.42%). 0.63% commission+spread +
1.08% execution lag = 1.71%, which brackets the 1.58% the equity identity
implies. Two unrelated methods agree within ~0.13pp, so this is not an artifact
of one bad trade.

**Consequence: the volatility floor is calibrated on commission when the real
cost is 2.5x that.** At the honest number the floor should be **1.88%**, and
today only **3 of 14** coins clear it (UNI 2.14, USELESS 4.04, MOG 3.39) versus
**10 of 14** at 0.75%. The seven coins in between — SOL, XRP, DOGE, LINK, DOT,
PEPE, FARTCOIN — are being admitted to trades they cannot pay for. XRP, today's
one fill, sits at 1.25% ATR: it spends ~42% of 1R on round-trip costs, well past
the 28% `MAX_FEE_DRAG_R` was written to cap.

That reframes "no demonstrated edge" (15 live days). The entry rule may not be
the only problem; the bot may be systematically taking trades with negative
expectancy *by construction*, because the cost model under-filters by 2.5x.

**Caveat, stated plainly:** this is inferred from an equity identity across 12
closed trades, not from per-fill commission data. Indodax fills report their own
commission but the ledger does not persist it (`ledger.py:63` stores gross
`(exit − entry) × qty` only). **The concrete next step is to capture per-fill
commission and actual fill price, which settles fee-vs-slippage decomposition
directly instead of by inference.** No config change shipped today — this
belongs to the improvement routine, on measured fills, not to a digest.
