### Reporting trap hit today

The routine's step-4c command calls `broker.get_account()` **without**
`price_by_symbol`, so it printed **equity Rp252.575 — the cash balance —
while two open positions were worth ~Rp255.000**. On a live-money bot
that reads as a 50% drawdown that did not happen. `scorecard.py` already
guards against this (it passes `replay._closes(...)` as marks and says so
in a comment); the daily routine does not. Figures above are marked to
the 16:00 UTC 1H closes. The routine prompt should be amended to pass
marks, or to report cash and equity as separate lines.
