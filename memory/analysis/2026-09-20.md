## The finding that matters: `risk_off`'s green gate is vacuous at 00:xx

The one position the hint admitted today is PEPE, entered 00:14. Replaying that
same 00:07 snapshot through the live gates rejects it — `risk_off: not green on
day`. The live bot took it. The difference is not a defect, it is the
measurement:

`trader.fetch_extras` (trader.py:56-61) sets `day_change_pct` from the **1D
bars**, as `(last 1D close / previous 1D close − 1)`. At 00:14 UTC "last 1D
close" is a bar **fourteen minutes old**, so the number is the move since
yesterday's close measured over a quarter of an hour. It is a coin flip around
zero. PEPE cleared it by a hair, and then fell **−3.26%** on the day and sits at
−0.47R.

So on the 00:xx cycle, `risk_off`'s strongest and most distinctive filter — the
one the regime_lab result was actually about — carries roughly no information,
while the same gate correctly blocked AVAX for 6 of the day's other hours. The
hint is at its weakest exactly where the day's only trade came through.

This is a code-level measurement question (measure against the UTC day open, or
let the 00:xx cycle inherit the previous bar), it is a strategy change, and it
does **not** belong in a policy file. Logged, not shipped — same handling as
yesterday's `half=` harness defect, which also remains unfixed.

## Stops: the correlated exit, priced

Yesterday's analysis named it: *"correlation ~1 is itself the risk — every open
position exits on one reversal."* Today, three of them did, inside ten minutes.

| XRP | −1.31R | −1.13R | 0.18R | +0.13R |

| LINK | −1.06R | −0.95R | 0.12R | −0.05R |

Two things to read here, and only one of them is a problem.

1. **A "1R" stop costs 1.06–1.31R in practice.** Fees are 0.12–0.18R of that
   and are structural. The rest is gap-through: the watchdog samples every 5
   minutes, and XRP was already 0.69% below its stop (Rp24.191 vs Rp24.358)
   when the check fired. The watcher then filled in 3 seconds. **The watchdog
   is not the lag — the sampling interval is**, and closing it is a code change.
2. **The cap worked as designed.** 4 slots × halved `risk_off` size = 3.0%
   aggregate risk; the realised hit was 3.1% of equity. That is the arithmetic
   yesterday's 2→4 change was built on, doing exactly what it said it would.
   The circuit breaker (4%) did not trip and should not have.

`max_positions` 4.

**Held `risk_off` for a fifth day.** The engine said `risk_on` all 17 cycles and
the account is 18.8pp behind a hold — that is precisely the pressure the rule
exists to resist. Nothing measured today refutes the entry filter (which *was*
tested at full size and won in all three regime_lab windows); today's finding
says the filter is **weaker than modelled at 00:xx**, which argues for less
confidence in loosening, not more. `regime_hint` can only tighten, so going
`auto` would release four gates at once on real money on the day the true win
rate hit its floor.

**Held `max_positions` at 4** rather than cutting to 3. Today's −3.1% was the
modelled outcome of 4 half-size slots, not a surprise; cutting the cap after one
realisation of exactly the loss the design predicts is reacting to a draw. The
circuit breaker is the backstop for this and it did not trip.
