## Finding: DOT was exited on a price the live watcher never saw

DOT entry Rp21.983, stop Rp20.908 (1R = Rp1.075). Exit reason `stop`, filled
Rp21.165 = **-0.76R** — i.e. the fill was Rp257 *above* the stop, which a stop
exit should not produce.

The 1H closes explain the trigger and indict it:

```
12:00  21.568
13:00  21.259
14:00  20.088   <- 1.76R below entry, fires the stop
15:00  20.762
16:00  21.137
```

The 14:00 close of **Rp20.088** is the only sub-stop value in the hourly series.
Three things say it was not a price the market actually held:

1. The market sell 14 minutes later filled at **Rp21.165 — 5.4% higher**.
2. The **5-minute watchdog logged no DOT breach at any tick**, in that hour or
   any other. It caught DOGE 03:30, LINK 07:35, LTC 13:10 the same day, so it
   was alive and holding DOT the whole time.
3. In the archived 1m bars, DOT traded at or below the stop in **exactly one
   minute of the day — 05:35, low = close = 20.088**. The identical rupiah
   value. (The 1m archive only reaches ~07:40; the 14:40 cron had not appended
   the afternoon yet, so the 14:00 hour cannot be checked directly.)

So the exit trigger was a single uncorroborated 1H print, equal to the rupiah to
a one-minute wick recorded nine hours earlier, that the live ticker disagreed
with at every 5-minute sample in the hour. Had the fill matched the signal the
trade would have been **-1.76R / ~-Rp19.000**; slippage handed back Rp11.000 and
it closed -0.76R instead.

**Generalise: the hourly stop check acts on one 1H close with no corroboration,
while the 5-minute watcher — the component actually observing live prices — is
never consulted to confirm it.** Today the two disagreed for a full hour and the
uncorroborated one won. That is the exact structural hole MOG's delisting note
described from the other side (fill 3.3R *past* the stop); this time the gap
between signal price and fill price ran in the account's favour. **It is
symmetric noise, not a one-way cost** — the month now has one costly instance
(MOG) and one profitable one (DOT).

No change proposed. The geometry is registered, the window is open, and "consult
the watchdog before trusting a lone print" is a strategy change the hourly
snapshot history cannot backtest. Logged as backlog, consistent with the
09-25/09-26/09-27 precedent of recording defects rather than editing mid-window.
