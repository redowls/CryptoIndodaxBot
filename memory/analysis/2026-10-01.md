single snapshot.**

breach at Rp21.716 — 0.18% under the stop — and the market sell filled

signal-price-vs-fill-price asymmetry logged on 09-27 (MOG, 3.3R past) and 09-28
(DOT, 1.0R short), today on the costly side. Mechanism working, cost real.
