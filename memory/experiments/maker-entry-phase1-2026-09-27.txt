=== VERDICT AGAINST THE PRE-REGISTERED GATE ===

Registered before the run, both required:
  1. mechanics sample: net entry-cost improvement >= +10 bps per trade
  2. signal sample: drift on non-fills does not exceed the saving on fills

  1. FAILS. Best bucket anywhere is +5.4 bps (very wide spread, 30m wait), over
     2.352 observations. The two buckets holding most of the watchlist sit at
     +0.3 and +3.0 bps. Two buckets are NEGATIVE at short waits.
  2. Passes, on 16 trades — and weakly. At 5m the MEDIAN trade gains -0.0 bps
     while the mean says +11.3, so over half of them gain nothing and a few
     carry the average. One coin (MOG, 1,78% spread) is 41% of the 15m result.

BOTH were required. The gate FAILS. Nothing ships to a live order path.

=== THE STRUCTURAL FINDING, WHICH IS WORTH MORE THAN THE VERDICT ===

There is no sweet spot, and the reason is not bad luck:

  wide spreads  -> big prize, but 12% fill at 5m, and NEGATIVE net at 5-10m
  tight spreads -> 44-66% fill, but the prize is ~3 bps

The prize and the fill rate are inversely related by construction. To fill at
the bid, price must come down to you; the entry signal is momentum breaking up.
Wide spreads mean a large prize and a rare fill. Tight spreads mean a reliable
fill worth almost nothing. Averaging across them does not produce an edge, it
produces a rounding error.

=== WHAT WOULD MAKE A RE-TEST LEGITIMATE (NOT A RE-ROLL) ===

The weakest input is the spread estimate: it rests on 13 observations recorded
today, applied to seven days of history. Phase 0 now records quotes hourly, so
in 2-3 weeks the same lab runs on real per-hour spreads and roughly 35 entries
instead of 16. That is new information, not a second roll of the same dice.

A methodological caveat, recorded because noticing it AFTER seeing which arm
failed is exactly what pre-registration exists to stop: the mechanics sample
prices a bid resting at a RANDOM hour, while the strategy rests one when the
entry signal fires. If the signal changes fill dynamics, mechanics is a biased
proxy. This is a reason the experiment is worth repeating with better data. It
is NOT a reason to override the gate, and it was not used as one.

=== ADDENDUM, same day: THE VERDICT IS FRAGILE IN BOTH DIRECTIONS ===

Six more spread observations (13 -> 19, still all from one afternoon) moved the
mechanics headline from +5.3 bps to +9.1 — most of the way to the +10 gate,
from nothing but a re-estimate of the spread.

Scaling every spread by a fifth:

    spreads x0.8   mechanics best +13.9 bps   PASSES the gate
    spreads x1.0   mechanics best  +9.1 bps   fails
    spreads x1.2   mechanics best  +8.6 bps   fails

So the whole result is dominated by its weakest input. The registered FAIL
stands — both legs were required and mechanics does not clear the bar on the
data in hand — but it should be read as "not demonstrated", not as "refuted". A
PASS built on the same estimate would have been just as weak, and would have
sent real money at a live order path.

This is the strongest argument yet for the 2026-10-18 re-test: by then the
spread input is thousands of hourly observations instead of nineteen from one
afternoon, and the answer will be worth its confidence either way.
